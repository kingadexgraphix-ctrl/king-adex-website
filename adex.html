About Us

King Adex Art & Graphics is a professional graphics and printing company based in Imota, Ikorodu, Lagos. We specialize in creative designs, branding, high-quality printing, flex banners, logo creation, custom frames, pencil portraits, jotters, calendars, and many other printing solutions.

Our mission is to provide quality, creativity, and excellent customer service while helping businesses and individuals bring their ideas to life.
